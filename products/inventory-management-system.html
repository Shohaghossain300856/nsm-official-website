Inventory Management System

Real-time supply chain, stock control, procurement, and accounting integration ERP.

Supply Chain ERP

Multi-Warehouse Stock Control

The **Inventory Management System by NSM Limited** delivers end-to-end stock visibility, barcode/RFID tracking, automated reorder thresholds, procurement order matching, sales distribution, and financial accounting ledger integration.

Core Feature Modules (Attachment Specs):

1. Product & Stock Management

Barcode scanning, SKU batch tracking, serial numbers, reorder points, & warehouse transfers.

2. Procurement & Supplier Management

Purchase orders, vendor evaluation, stock receiving logs, & 3-way invoice matching.

3. Sales & Distribution Management

Sales order processing, wholesale dispatching, return logs, & customer pricing tiers.

4. Financial & Accounting Integration

Automated ledger posting, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) calculation, & VAT/tax compliance.

5. Reporting, Analytics & System Control

Real-time valuation, fast-moving items forecast, role permission security, & audit logs.
