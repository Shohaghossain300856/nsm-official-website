Corporate Engineering & Innovation

**NSM Limited** is a registered Private Limited Company under the **Registrar of Joint Stock Companies and Firms (RJSC)** in Bangladesh, an official member of **BASIS**, and an accredited **Crossref Sponsoring Organization**.
